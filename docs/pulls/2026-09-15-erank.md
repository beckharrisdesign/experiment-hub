## Distilled findings — tag report

**1. Of the 287 tags in use, eRank scores difficulty for only 135 — and 128 of those are difficulty 100.** The seven scored below 100: `digital products` (35), `digital product` (70), `self care gift` (75), `embroidery designs` (77), `photo ornament` (95), `gardening shirt` (95), `stocking stuffer` (97) — the sub-80 four are also among the highest-volume tags in use. The remaining 152 tags (largely the newer coined/positioning sets) carry no difficulty score at all, so no claim can be made about them from this report.

**2. Measured search volume lives almost entirely in generic and gift terms.** Volume leaders across current tags: `personalized gift` 41.4K avg searches, `digital products` 31.2K, `housewarming gift` 13.6K, `christmas ornament` 9.2K, `embroidery designs` 7.1K, `stocking stuffer` 6.2K, `self care gift` 5.8K, `best friend gift` 4.1K.

**3. The niche/positioning tags have essentially zero measured volume.** Nearly every craft-specific and coined tag reads "< 20 searches" or "Unknown" — including most of the new treatment sets (diamond wreath, geometric wheel, leaf fan…) and holiday sets. eRank simply has no data for them.

**4. The honest tension with our own data — recorded, not resolved.** eRank's market view (volume + low difficulty in generic terms, e.g. `digital products`) points the opposite way from our shop's own ads data (`digital products`: 28 impressions, 0 clicks) and search-visit evidence (specific descriptors convert). Both can be true: eRank measures marketwide demand, not fit to this shop's products; its CTR column (clicks÷searches, often >100%) is a market metric, not our listings' CTR. **This is exactly what the tag-positioning experiment adjudicates — no copy changes now.** At day 30: if treatment listings with zero-volume descriptor tags gained views, position-beats-volume holds here; if they stayed flat, this report argues for reallocating 2–3 tag slots per listing to the four low-difficulty/high-volume terms.

**5. One immediate note for the day-30 rollout menu:** `self care gift` (5.8K searches, difficulty 75) is already the wellness thesis in market-demand form — if wellness positioning wins, it's the highest-leverage tag to spread. `embroidery designs` (7.1K, difficulty 77) is the strongest broad craft term by this data, consistent with it being mid-tier (not bottom) in our ads panels.
