# Data pull — Etsy Ads daily stats, Jun 25 – Jul 25, 2026, captured 2026-07-25

**Provenance:** Katy's export from Shop Manager → Advertising → Stats (the `etsy_ads_stats_<start>_<end>.csv` download), captured 2026-07-25, found un-landed in `~/Downloads` on 2026-09-21 and landed as `2026-07-25-etsy-ads-stats-2026-06-25-2026-07-25.csv`. Not read by the ingest — there is no daily-ads reader — so this is for reading and for the ledger.

## What surface this is

Campaign-level, per day: the only Etsy surface that gives the ad programme a time axis. The per-listing keyword tables ([9/20](2026-09-20-etsy-ads-listing-keywords.md), [9/21](2026-09-21-etsy-ads-listing-keywords.md)) say *which terms*; this says *when*.

## Distilled findings

**1. 31 days, 377 views, 5 clicks, 1 order, $2.49 revenue on $0.88 spend.** Views landed on 9 of the 31 days; the rest read zero — the campaign was serving intermittently, not daily. The one order is Jul 24, the leaf mandala's $2.49 sale.

**2. Nothing here changes a decision on its own.** It is the earliest daily point in the series; the [Jan–Sep export](2026-09-17-etsy-ads-stats.md) supersedes it for every question except "what did July look like on the day".
