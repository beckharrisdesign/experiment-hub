## What the data says

**Views turn into sales in this shop.** The three probable sales (Jul 25, Aug 16, Sep 3) are on exactly the three most-viewed listings. Roughly 1 sale per 40–80 views. So the plan is: get more views to listings that earn them, and fix the listings getting none.

**The rubric's verdict is consistent:** every listing passes Etsy's required fields; every listing has the same recurring gaps — image slots unfilled (most at 10 of 20), titles short of the 120–140 sweet spot, thin alt text, and no video (1 of 27 has one — and it's the highest-scoring listing in the shop).
