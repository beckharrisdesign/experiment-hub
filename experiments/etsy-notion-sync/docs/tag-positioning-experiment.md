# Tag positioning experiment

**Question:** Do distinct positioning keywords (one clear primary keyword per listing, wellness/gift framing, title + tags moving together) outperform generic craft-category keywords and cloned tag sets — in organic search and in Etsy Ads targeting?

**Why title + tags together:** Two listings (4415081378, 4466797252) already carry a near-verbatim copy of the leaf mandala's wellness tag cluster and got 1 view each in 30 days, while the mandala got 46. Tags copied onto an undifferentiated listing don't rescue it — the treatment is the whole keyword position: a unique primary keyword in the title, backed by a tag set no sibling listing duplicates. Etsy Ads derives targeted keywords from title/tags, so the same edit moves both channels.

## Copy rules (every treatment edit follows these)

1. **Title formula:** `Hand Embroidery Pattern PDF — [unique descriptor] — [positioning phrase]`. Lead with the searched shape; "digital" leads nowhere — no one searched it.
2. **One unique primary descriptor per listing** (geometric wheel, leaf fan, diamond wreath…) — no descriptor reused across listings.
3. **Format words inside title phrases only** ("pattern pdf", "pdf download") — never as standalone tags. Strip "digital products", "digital product", "instant download", "printable pdf" from treatment tag sets.
4. **No tag appears on more than one treatment listing's set** unless it's a deliberate shared foundation ("hand embroidery pattern" class); the cloned wellness cluster gets broken up.
5. **One skill level per listing** — beginner OR intermediate OR advanced, in title and tags consistently.
6. **Wellness/gift positioning where the design supports it** (calm, slow stitching, self care gift) — as the positioning phrase, not as the primary keyword.

## Measurement

Two channels, both anchored to the day edits land:

**Organic (automatic):** the daily sync snapshots title, tags, views, favorites — intervention date and every delta recorded with no new code. 30-day window, day-14 sanity check. Compare treatment vs control mean views/30d and favorites; confirm protected listings didn't dip.

**Ads (manual bookends):** at day 30, re-capture the same panels — the 7 baseline rows above plus each treatment listing's panel. Look for (a) targeted keywords shifting from generic terms toward each listing's new specific descriptor, and (b) CTR on treatment listings' keywords moving off 0%. Also re-export Search Analytics (Shop Manager → Stats → Search analytics) for the organic term list.

## Verdict rule

- **Positioning wins:** treatment mean ≥ 2× control mean views/30d, or ≥ 2 treatment listings reach 10+ views/30d → roll the approach across the rest of the catalog (including protected listings' ad-keyword pruning, which waits until then).
- **Secondary signal:** treatment ad panels re-target on the new specific descriptors with any nonzero CTR.
- **Flat:** both groups stay ~0–2 views and ads keep serving generic terms → keywords aren't the constraint; next hypothesis is thumbnails/photos.
- Caveat: n=6 per group, single-digit views, sub-dollar ad spend — directional, not statistical.
