Standard executive-function and working-memory assessments, digitized faithfully
and tracked over time — a measurement instrument, not another organizational tool.

Provenance: prompted by talks at the Shimmer annual conference. The deliberate
constraint is that v1 implements the *industry-standard* protocols correctly
before any spatial-topological reformatting is attempted — the standard version
is the baseline the v2 variants will have to be measured against.

Explicitly not: journaling, task management, AI coaching, or diagnosis.
